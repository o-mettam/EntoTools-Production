Privacy Policy

How EntoTools handles your data. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

1. Overview

EntoTools ("we", "us", the "Service") is a free set of browser-based tools for entomological data, growing degree day (GDD) calculations, and specimen label generation, available at entotools.org. We are committed to collecting as little personal data as possible.

Every core feature works without an account. By default, the data you enter is stored only in your own web browser. An account (Section 3) is optional; data reaches our servers only if you create one, use a feature that needs a server lookup (Section 4), or send feedback (Section 5).

2. Data Stored in Your Browser

Your label collection database, saved searches, cached results, and theme preference are stored in your browser's localStorage on your device.

  • Collection database: stored under the key entoLabelSheetV2.
  • Preferences and cache: theme setting and cached search results / saved searches.

You can delete this data at any time using the Clear Local Cache control in the Settings menu, or by clearing your browser's site data. If you are signed in to an account, your collection is also kept in your account until you delete it there (Section 3).

3. Optional Account

You can create an account to sign in with a passkey (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, a security key, or your password manager) and keep your collection in sync across devices. We store the following on our servers, which are hosted by Cloudflare:

  • Email address — the address you sign up with, used to identify your account and for support (for example, if you lose your passkey). We do not send marketing email.
  • Passkeys — for each passkey, its public key and ID, a usage counter, the name you give it, and when it was added and last used. Your fingerprint, face, PIN, or private key never leave your device; we cannot see them.
  • Sign-in sessions — when each session started, when it was last used, and which passkey opened it. A session ends after 7 days without use and after 30 days at most.
  • Synced collection — while you are signed in, the Label Data and Collection Database pages sync your collection database (including collection localities, coordinates, collectors, and notes) to your account.
  • Feature settings — whether optional or early-access features are turned on for your account.

A small number of site administrators can look up an account by email to help with support. They can see the email address, passkey names, and session dates, and can remove passkeys or sessions, delete an account, or issue a one-time link to add a new passkey. Every administrator action is recorded in an internal log.

We never sell or share account data, and never use it for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

To provide weather and location features, the Service sends the location and date information you search for (such as coordinates or place names) to the following services. Some lookups go through our server; others are made directly from your browser, in which case the service also sees your IP address. We never send your name, email, or account details to them.

  • NOAA NCEI — historical US weather data.
  • Open-Meteo — historical/forecast weather, elevation, humidity.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — geocoding of place names and coordinates.
  • Sunrise-Sunset.org — sunset times.

Pages also load a few files from these providers, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of any normal web request:

  • jsDelivr — open-source code libraries (passkey sign-in, charts, maps).
  • Google Fonts — web fonts.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles, only when you open the collection map.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery, security, and the account database.

Each third party processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. Feedback

If you submit feedback through the Service, what you write is posted as a publicly visible issue on our GitHub project so we can review and improve the tools. You do not need an account to send feedback.

Email is optional. If you enter one so we can reply, it is shown on the public issue, as the feedback form explains. Leave it blank to stay anonymous.

Bug reports automatically include the app version, page, theme, your browser's user-agent string, and recent console logs from this site; this is noted on the feedback form. Before anything is posted, your message and these logs are automatically filtered to remove email addresses, IP addresses, coordinates, and credential-like strings, and the logs do not record your saved-search names, labels, or file names. Even so, please do not include sensitive personal information in feedback.

6. Cookies and Tracking

The Service does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or cross-site tracking. It sets only these cookies, both limited to entotools.org:

  • __Host-ento_session — keeps you signed in, only if you have an account. It cannot be read by page scripts and is removed when you sign out.
  • ento_su — counts accounts created from your browser, to limit abuse. It expires after 2 hours.

Your browser's localStorage is used as described in Section 2. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may also set security cookies needed to protect the site.

7. Security, Logs, and Retention

  • Abuse limits: to stop spam and overload, we count requests per network for up to 2 hours. Your IP address is converted with a keyed one-way hash before it is counted, so it is never stored in readable form.
  • Server logs: our server code does not log your searched locations, and we do not keep a long-term request log. Cloudflare processes standard request logs to run and secure the network.
  • Caches: geocoding results are cached for 24 hours. Historical weather for the "season vs. normal" view is cached by rounded location (about 1 km) for up to 90 days. These caches are not linked to you or your account.
  • Accounts: account data is kept until you delete your account. Expired sign-in sessions are removed automatically. When an account is deleted, its email, passkeys, sessions, settings, and synced collection are deleted; an administrator's action log may keep the email address of an account they deleted.

8. Your Choices

If you have an account, open Settings → Manage account to:

  • Download my data — a JSON file (account email, passkey names and dates, feature settings, and your synced collection).
  • Sign out everywhere else — ends your sessions on other devices.
  • Rename or remove passkeys.
  • Delete my account — removes it and everything stored with it. This cannot be undone.

Data stored only in your browser can be removed with Clear Local Cache or your browser's settings. For anything else, contact us (Section 11).

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for general, educational, and research use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
